# Syed Raza

**Visiting Assistant Professor of Sociology, Kenyon College**

I study how religious and ethical commitments shape economic life.

## Research

My dissertation, *Divine Dollars: Classificatory Struggles of Islamic Finance in Chicago*,
draws on ethnographic fieldwork to examine how Muslims decide which financial
practices are religiously permissible.

## Teaching

At Kenyon, I teach Sociology of Religion and Contesting India.
