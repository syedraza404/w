Syed Raza

Visiting Assistant Professor of Sociology, Kenyon College

I study how religion and moral judgment shape economic life.

My research follows questions about faith and finance into everyday decisions about investing and borrowing. In Chicago, I examine how Muslims weigh religious guidance, financial expertise, and obligations to their communities when deciding which practices they can participate in.
